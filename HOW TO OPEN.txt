Team Odyssey - HN LoRaWAN Planner pitch
=======================================

1. Keep this folder together (the .html file and the "assets" folder side by side).
   You can copy or move the whole folder anywhere, to any computer or a USB stick.
2. Double-click "Team Odyssey Pitch.html". It opens in any web browser
   (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). No internet and no installation needed.

Controls
  Right arrow / Space / mouse wheel / swipe   next step
  Left arrow                                  previous step
  F                                           fullscreen
  N                                           speaker notes
  K                                           play / pause the demo video
  Buttons under the video jump to a chapter.

Sending it to someone: send "Team Odyssey Pitch.zip" (next to this folder) and tell them
to unzip it first, then double-click the .html file inside.
