The challenge

Water meters send their readings by radio.
An antenna has to catch them.

1 · The meter

Every home has a water meter. It sends its reading by radio.

2 · The antenna

An antenna on a rooftop (a "gateway") catches the signal.

3 · HNVG

The water company gets the reading. Nobody has to knock on the door.

The problem

Buildings and hills block the radio signal. With today's 8 antennas, about half of the homes are not heard and are still read by hand.

Our task

HNVG has approved 71 places for new antennas. We pick the fewest of them that still hear almost every meter.

What we built

The planner, live on Heilbronn

Why us

What makes it unique

Backup for every Gateway

Most homes are heard by two or more antennas. If one goes down, another one still catches the reading.

Future proof

We add the new buildings from the government's official building plans, so the antennas still work when new districts are built.

Works in any city

Tested on Stuttgart using open data only. For any other city: just drop in its data and run it.
